# Get help

The quickest way to get help is our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/9G9H4HdtJx). Ask questions, share feedback and report bugs there.

Before asking, check the [FAQ](/help/faq) and [Agent troubleshooting](/agent/troubleshooting). Your answer might already be there.

## Report a bug

A good bug report helps us fix things faster. Include:

- **What happened**, and what you expected to happen.
- **Steps to reproduce it**, if you can.
- **Your Tinker Dash version.**
- **Agent output:** any error the Agent printed, or the relevant lines from its logs.
- **Server details:** server type (Vanilla, Paper, Fabric or NeoForge) and Minecraft version.
- **Console lines** around the problem, copied from the [console](/dash/console).
- **Screenshots**, if something looks wrong.

:::danger[Never share your token]
Remove your Agent token from anything you post. Anyone with it can control your servers.
:::
