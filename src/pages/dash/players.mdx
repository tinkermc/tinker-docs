# Players

The **Players** tab lists everyone who has joined your server and lets you moderate them in one click.

## Player lists

Players are grouped by status:

- **Online**: playing right now.
- **Offline**: has joined before but isn't on now.
- **Banned**: can't join until unbanned.

Select a player to see their details and actions.

## Kick, ban and unban

- **Kick** removes a player from the server. They can rejoin straight away.
- **Ban** removes the player and stops them from joining again.
- **Unban** lets a banned player join again.

When you kick or ban, you can add a reason. The player sees it when they're removed, so keep it clear and short.

## Operator and whitelist

- **Operator** (OP) gives a player full access to server commands. Only give it to people you trust.
- **Whitelist** controls who is allowed to join. When the whitelist is on, only players on it can join.

Use the switches on a player to turn operator or whitelist on and off. To let in someone who has never joined, use **Add to whitelist** and enter their Minecraft username.

:::warning
Turn the whitelist on *after* adding yourself and your friends, or you may lock everyone out.
:::

## Player notes

You can write private notes on any player, such as why they were warned. Notes are saved on your PC only. They aren't sent to the player or the server, and they won't appear in Dash on another computer.
